Registration & Payment

Registration & Payment Process

  • Page Allowance: Every accepted paper can have up to 6 pages in the final PDF. If needed, additional pages (maximum 2 more additional pages apart from basic 6 pages) can be added to make the final version of the paper. This requires paying an extra charge of INR 2,000 per additional page at the time of author registration payment.
  • Confirmation: The participant must complete the registration and payment process via the official registration form after receiving paper acceptance notification.
  • Inclusions: The registration fee includes a conference delegate kit, access to all technical keynote sessions and tracks, networking lunches, and high tea/coffee refreshments.
  • Receipts: Please record transaction UTR numbers and preserve screenshots of payment confirmation, which must be uploaded with the registration form.
