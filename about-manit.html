About MANIT

MANIT Bhopal

Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT) Bhopal is a prestigious Institution of National Importance located in the heart of India. Formerly known as Maulana Azad College of Technology (MACT), MANIT has a rich history of providing quality technical education and significantly contributing to the scientific and technological development of the nation.

Sprawling over an expansive 650-acre green campus, MANIT provides state-of-the-art infrastructure, world-class research laboratories, and modern computational facilities that foster innovation and academic excellence.

History and Legacy

Established in 1960, MANIT was jointly sponsored by the Government of India and the Government of Madhya Pradesh as one of the pioneering Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs). The institute was named in honor of the esteemed scholar, visionary leader, and the first Education Minister of independent India, Maulana Abul Kalam Azad.

In 2002, the institute was upgraded to National Institute of Technology (NIT) status and declared an Institute of National Importance under the NIT Act by the Parliament of India. Since its inception, MANIT has been dedicated to producing highly skilled, talented, and ethical manpower across various engineering, sciences, architecture, and management domains.

Academic Programs

MANIT offers a wide range of academic programs to nurture the next generation of innovators, researchers, and industry leaders:

  • Undergraduate Programs: B.Tech in major engineering disciplines and Bachelor of Architecture & Planning.
  • Postgraduate Programs: M.Tech, MCA, MBA, and M.Sc. degrees designed for specialized industry and research expertise.
  • Doctoral Programs: Ph.D. research across interdisciplinary frontiers in sciences, engineering, computing, and humanities.

Commitment to Excellence

MANIT is steadfastly committed to excellence in teaching, sponsored research, and technological innovation. The institute strives to provide a conducive learning ecosystem that encourages intellectual curiosity, creativity, and lifelong learning. With a strong alumni network spread globally across leading academia, research institutes, and multinational corporations, MANIT continues to be at the forefront of national education.

For more comprehensive information about MANIT Bhopal, please visit the official institutional portal: https://www.manit.ac.in/
