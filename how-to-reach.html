How to Reach MANIT Bhopal

Travel & Transit Guide

Bhopal is centrally located and exceptionally well-connected by air, rail, and road networks across India

✈️

Arrival by Air

Bhopal's Raja Bhoj International Airport (BHO) is located approximately 15 km from the MANIT campus. It offers frequent domestic flights connecting to major transit hubs including New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad.

Local Transit: Pre-paid airport taxis and app-based cab services (Ola/Uber) are readily available outside the arrivals terminal.

🚆

Arrival by Train

Bhopal is a pivotal junction on the Indian Railways network. Key railway stations include Bhopal Junction (BPL) (~8 km) and the state-of-the-art Rani Kamlapati Railway Station (RKMP) (~6 km). Both stations are served by Vande Bharat, Rajdhani, Shatabdi, and Express trains.

Local Transit: Auto-rickshaws, pre-paid taxis, and city buses connect both stations directly to MANIT.

🚗

Arrival by Road

Bhopal is connected by a well-maintained network of National (NH-46, NH-146) and State Highways to major cities including Indore (190 km), Ujjain (190 km), Jabalpur (300 km), and Nagpur (350 km).

Interstate Buses: Frequent AC sleeper and deluxe buses operate from ISBT Bhopal and Nadra Bus Stand.

Reaching the Campus Inside Bhopal

MANIT is situated along Link Road Number 3 in the heart of South Bhopal, near Mata Mandir and Kaliasot Dam. When taking a taxi or auto-rickshaw from the airport or railway station, ask for "MANIT Main Gate, Link Road No. 3".
