Call for Papers

Submission Invitation

The International Conference on Secure Computing, Computational Mechanics & Business Analytics (ICSCMBA - 2026) warmly invites researchers, academicians, scientists, postgraduate scholars, and industry professionals to submit original, high-quality research contributions.

This conference provides an esteemed academic platform for sharing innovative ideas, groundbreaking experimental findings, and real-world implementations in the interconnected spheres of secure computing, computational mechanics, statistical modeling, machine learning, and data analytics.

Themes of ICSCMBA - 2026

Integrating Security, Computational Modeling, and Digital Business Transformation

Track 1: Secure Computing & Computational Intelligence

  • Cryptography & Security Protocols
  • Blockchains & Distributed Ledgers
  • Distributed & Parallel Systems
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC)
  • Cloud & Edge Computing
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Data Science & Big Data Analytics
  • AI & Machine Learning / Deep Learning
  • Quantum Computing

Track 2: Computational Mechanics & Engineering Systems

  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
  • Numerical Methods for Engineering
  • Multiphysics Modeling & Simulation
  • AI in Engineering Simulations
  • Computational Biomechanics
  • Energy Harvesting Systems
  • MEMS & NEMS Technologies

Track 3: Business Analytics & Management Science

  • Machine Learning for Finance & Econometrics
  • Blockchain in FinTech
  • Operations Research & Optimization
  • Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics
  • Intelligent Decision Support Systems
  • Risk & Financial Fraud Analytics
  • HR & Marketing Analytics

Full-Length Paper Submission

Authors are invited to submit full-length papers strictly adhering to the Springer proceedings formatting guidelines. Submissions must be uploaded online electronically by the submission deadline. At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper for it to be included in the conference proceedings.

View Author Guidelines & Templates Submit via Microsoft CMT

Publication & Indexing

Conference proceedings will be published by Springer in the Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS) series. Springer CCIS volumes are indexed in Scopus, DBLP, Google Scholar, EI-Compendex, Mathematical Reviews, and SCImago, and submitted for inclusion in ISI Proceedings.

Important Note on Quality & Plagiarism Policy

Accepted papers must pass Springer's rigorous technical and ethical quality checks prior to final publication. Springer Nature does not charge publication fees for non-open-access proceedings content. Short papers (less than 10 pages) or extended abstracts will not be considered for publication.

Plagiarism Threshold

* Only original manuscripts with an overall similarity index of strictly below 20% (verified via institutional plagiarism detection tools) will be considered for peer review and further publication processing.
