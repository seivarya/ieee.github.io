About Conference

ICSCMBA - 2026

The International Conference on Secure Computing, Computational Mechanics & Business Analytics (ICSCMBA - 2026) is a premier forum for the exchange of cutting-edge research and ideas in the fields of mathematics, engineering mechanics, secure computing, and data analytics.

This conference aims to bring together faculty, scientists, researchers, and students from both industry and academia to share their latest findings and advancements. ICSCMBA - 2026 will be held in hybrid mode, offering both virtual and in-person participation options, ensuring accessibility for a global audience.

Conference Objectives

ICSCMBA - 2026 has the following key objectives:

  • To provide a platform for students and researchers to disseminate the latest research in mathematics, secure computing, and data science.
  • To foster collaboration and networking among researchers, scientists, and industry professionals across disciplines.
  • To promote innovation and advancements in the theoretical and practical applications of computational intelligence and analytics.
  • To facilitate discussions on emerging trends and challenges in these dynamic fields.
  • To offer opportunities for professional development through keynote presentations, invited lectures, and research papers.

Conference Highlights

ICSCMBA - 2026 will feature a diverse range of academic activities, including:

  • Keynote lectures by internationally renowned experts in secure computing, mechanics, and data science.
  • Invited talks and interactive panel discussions by distinguished researchers.
  • Technical paper presentation sessions showcasing peer-reviewed original research contributions.
  • Official presentation and participation certificates awarded to all registered authors and attendees.

Themes of ICSCMBA - 2026

The conference solicits contributions across a broad spectrum of research domains, categorized into major topic groups:

Computational Mathematics

  • Algebra, Linear Algebra & Geometry
  • Number Theory & Numerical Analysis
  • Mathematical Modeling & Fluid Dynamics
  • Financial Mathematics
  • Discrete Mathematics & Graph Theory
  • Fixed Point Theory & Fuzzy Mathematics
  • Probability & Statistics
  • Operations Research

Secure Computing & Systems

  • Blockchain Security & Applications
  • Cryptography & Coding Theory
  • Cyber Security & Privacy
  • Software Architecture & Design Patterns
  • High-Performance Computing (HPC)
  • Cloud & Edge Computing
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Next Generation Networks

Analytics & Intelligence

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Machine Learning & Deep Learning
  • Quantum Computing
  • Information Retrieval
  • Data Warehousing & Data Mining
  • Computer Vision & Image Processing
  • Big Data Analytics & Decision Systems

Publication Opportunities

Selected and accepted papers presented at ICSCMBA - 2026 will be submitted to Springer for possible publication in Springer Proceedings (CCIS Series) after a standard peer-review process.

Publication in Springer Proceedings is subject to the final peer-review decision and formatting guidelines of Springer.
