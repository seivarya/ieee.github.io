About Department

Department of Mathematics, Bioinformatics & Computer Applications

The Department of Mathematics, Bioinformatics & Computer Applications (MBC) at MANIT Bhopal is a dynamic academic centre committed to pushing the boundaries of knowledge in mathematical sciences, computing technologies, and their wide-ranging interdisciplinary applications.

The department fosters an environment of rigorous intellectual inquiry, high-impact research, and industry-aligned academic curriculum that equips students to become versatile problem-solvers and innovators.

Our Focus

The department emphasizes a strong tradition of excellence in teaching, research, and innovation. We are proud of our distinguished faculty, comprising accomplished academicians and researchers with expertise across core domains:

  • Pure & Applied Mathematics: Theoretical foundations, numerical analysis, differential equations, and mathematical physics.
  • Bioinformatics: Computational biology, genomic sequence analysis, structural modeling, and biomedical data analytics.
  • Computer Applications: Algorithms, cloud architectures, database systems, intelligent software engineering, and network security.

Research and Innovation

The department is actively involved in high-impact research, with dedicated research groups exploring frontier fields such as:

  • Cryptography, Network Security & Information Privacy
  • Blockchain Architectures & Distributed Ledger Technologies
  • Mathematical Modeling, Optimization & Simulation
  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Deep Learning
  • Bioinformatics, Computational Biology & Health Informatics
  • Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing & Internet of Things

To support advanced research, students and scholars have access to modern high-performance computational laboratories and specialized scientific software toolkits.

Our Commitment

We are committed to providing an innovative and industry-relevant curriculum that prepares our graduates to excel in an increasingly complex and technology-driven world. Our collaborative initiatives, national research grants, and global academic partnerships enhance the educational experience and ensure our graduates are exceptionally well-prepared to meet emerging challenges in science, engineering, and society.
