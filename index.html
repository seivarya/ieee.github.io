About the Conference

The Department of Mathematics, Bioinformatics, and Computer Applications is proud to announce the International Conference on Secure Computing, Computational Mechanics & Business Analytics (ICSCMBA - 2026), which will be organized in a hybrid mode — offering both virtual and in-person participation options.

The conference proceedings will be published in Springer - CCIS series (indexed in Scopus, SCImago, DBLP, Google Scholar).

This conference aims to provide a dynamic platform for students, scientists, researchers, and professionals from industry and academia to come together and share their latest research findings, innovative ideas, and technological advancements in the diverse fields of Mathematics, Computing, and Data Science. By bringing together a wide spectrum of participants, ICSCMBA - 2026 seeks to foster interdisciplinary collaborations, inspire new research directions, and strengthen the exchange of knowledge across these critical domains.

The conference program will feature a rich blend of activities, including keynote addresses by eminent speakers, invited talks by distinguished researchers, and paper presentation sessions where participants can showcase their original work. Certificates will be awarded to all registered participants who present their papers and attend the sessions.

Themes of ICSCMBA - 2026

Interdisciplinary tracks covering computing, engineering mechanics, and analytics

Track 1: Secure Computing & Computational Intelligence

  • Cryptography & Network Security
  • Blockchains & Distributed Ledgers
  • Distributed & Parallel Systems
  • High Performance Computing (HPC)
  • Cloud & Edge Computing
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Data Science & Big Data Analytics
  • AI & Machine Learning / Deep Learning
  • Quantum Computing

Track 2: Computational Mechanics & Engineering Systems

  • Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
  • Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
  • Numerical Methods for Engineering
  • Multiphysics Modeling
  • AI in Engineering Simulation
  • Computational Biomechanics & Energy Harvesting
  • MEMS & NEMS

Track 3: Business Analytics & Management Science

  • Machine Learning for Finance
  • Blockchain in FinTech
  • Operations Research & Optimization
  • Supply Chain Analytics
  • Decision Support Systems
  • Risk & Fraud Analytics
  • HR & Marketing Analytics
