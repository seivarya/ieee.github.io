Author Guidelines

Instructions for Preparing Manuscripts

Prospective authors are cordially invited to submit full-length original, unpublished research papers. In submitting a manuscript to ICSCMBA 2026, authors acknowledge that no paper substantially similar in content has been or will be submitted to another journal, conference, or workshop during the active review period. Duplicate or dual submissions will be rejected without peer review.

  • Submission Format: All manuscripts must be electronically submitted in PDF format via the Microsoft Research CMT portal before the deadline.
  • Page Limit: Authors should prepare manuscripts of 10 to 15 pages (including all text, equations, figures, tables, and references) adhering strictly to Springer-CCIS formatting standards.
  • File Constraints: The manuscript PDF file size must not exceed 10 MB. All fonts must be embedded and scalable.
  • Image Requirements: Embedded images must not contain transparent alpha-channels to ensure crisp rendering during printing and digital typesetting.
  • PDF Security: Ensure no Adobe Document Protection, watermarks, or security encryption is enabled on the submitted PDF.

Proceedings & Indexing

The proceedings of the conference will be published by Scopus-indexed Springer in the prestigious Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS) series (CCIS Series Details).

Indexing & Abstracting

Springer CCIS volumes are abstracted and indexed in major databases including DBLP, Google Scholar, EI-Compendex, Mathematical Reviews, SCImago, and Scopus, and submitted for inclusion in ISI Proceedings.

Preparing Your Proceedings Paper

Authors must structure and format their proceedings papers using the predefined Springer templates. Detailed guidelines are available directly from Springer: Springer Proceedings Guidelines for Authors.
